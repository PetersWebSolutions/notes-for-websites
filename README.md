# TEAM ELITE PH TSHIRT

Sales tally for Team Elite PH t-shirt orders.

Open `index.html` in a browser. Enter each person's name, size, and color. The price fills in from the size, and the total stays at the bottom of the list.

## Prices

Prices are in Philippine pesos.

| Size | Price |
| --- | --- |
| S, M, L, XL | ₱399 |
| 2XL | ₱449 |

White and blue are the same price. The size is written in the WHITE or BLUE column.

## List

- Up to 300 people, one shirt per row
- Add the same name again for another shirt
- Click PAID or UNPAID to update payment
- Totals include the full amount, what is paid, and what is still unpaid

Names stay in this browser. They are not uploaded. Use **Backup** if you need a copy, and **Export CSV** or **Copy summary** to share the count.
