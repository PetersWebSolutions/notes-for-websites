# TEAM ELITE PH TSHIRT

Sales tally for Team Elite PH t-shirt orders.

Open `index.html` in a browser. Add each person by name, then use their row to pick a size and quantity for white and for blue. Extra 3XL and 4XL shirts have their own columns. The price fills in from the sizes, and the people and shirt totals stay in the header and at the bottom of the list.

## Prices

Prices are in Philippine pesos.

| Size | Price |
| --- | --- |
| S, M, L, XL | ₱399 |
| 2XL, 3XL, 4XL | ₱449 |

White and blue are the same price. The size is picked under the WHITE or BLUE column.

## List

- Up to 300 people, one person per row
- One person can order both colors, each with its own size and quantity
- The 3XL and 4XL columns hold extra big-size shirts at ₱449 each
- Click PAID or UNPAID to update payment
- Totals show the people count, the shirt count, and what is paid and still unpaid
- The phone layout stacks each person into a compact two-line card

Tap **Save list** to store the list on this website (in this browser). It is not uploaded. Use **Backup** if you need a copy, and **Export CSV** or **Copy summary** to share the count.
